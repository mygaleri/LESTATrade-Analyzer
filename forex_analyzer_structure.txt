TEKNOLOGI YANG DIGUNAKAN:
- Frontend: Flutter (Dart)
- Backend: Node.js/Express
- Database: Firebase Realtime DB / MongoDB
- Real-time: WebSockets
- Charts: fl_chart
- State Management: Provider
- Local Storage: Hive/SQLite
